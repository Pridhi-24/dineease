About DineEase

Seasonal cooking, a warm room, your table waiting

DineEase is a neighbourhood restaurant built around unhurried evenings: candlelit tables, produce from nearby farms and a team that remembers how you like to be looked after.

Every table is assigned with care, so whether it is an anniversary for two or a family celebration for eight, you are seated exactly where the evening feels best.
